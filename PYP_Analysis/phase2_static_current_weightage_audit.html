Phase II: static, policy and current-affairs distribution

Manual demand audit of supplied 2021-2025 memory-based papers: 150 ESI objective items, 150 FM objective items, 60 offered descriptive questions and 20 offered essay topics. These are classifications of the supplied reconstructions, not an official RBI weightage.

Read the numbers correctly

  • ESI and FM: objective 50 marks plus descriptive 50 attempted marks. The compilations contain 30 objective items per subject/year but do not identify their individual 1-/2-mark weights. Consequently this audit does NOT invent category marks. Question-share percentages are not verified mark percentages.
  • Descriptive: three 15-mark and three 10-mark options give a 75-mark menu. The 50-mark attempt (two 15-mark and two 10-mark answers) must not be confused with that menu.
  • T = theory, enduring concepts, institutions or historical knowledge; R = standing scheme/product/regulatory/policy rules; D = a dated report finding, event, incumbent, current change or recent initiative. R is not automatically recent current affairs. Rules and values still need updating.
  • ESI economics includes production, trade, enterprise, monetary policy and agricultural income/productivity. Social includes health, education, social insurance, gender, caste and basic urban welfare. Environment/general global issues are shown separately. One primary category per question prevents double counting; boundary notes preserve overlap.
  • A report passage does not make every question current: WTO history, IMF functions, liquidity mechanisms and PVTG criteria remain static when those are the actual demands. Report identification and edition-specific metadata are policy/current-linked study tasks, even where the publisher itself is enduring knowledge.
  • 2019 is an older format; no 2020 paper was supplied; the supplied 2026 paper is Phase I only. These years are not pooled with the modern Phase II sample. No 2027 prediction is implied.

What can honestly be said about marks?

Exact objective category marks are unavailable from these sources. If a 30-question, 50-mark section has twenty 2-mark and ten 1-mark items, a category containing k questions has between k + max(0,k-10) and k + min(k,20) marks. These are conditional marginal bounds, not additive allocations. For example, 6 questions could represent 6-12 marks. Without question-wise weights, multiplying every category by 50/30 gives only an equal-weight normalisation, not observed historical marks; it is deliberately not presented as weightage here.

Preparation implications, not predicted marks

  • ESI: objective preparation must cover schemes, reports, policy design, eligibility and application cases, not just monthly headlines. Keep static economics and social frameworks for descriptive explanations. Integrate climate/development instead of forcing it wholly into economics or social issues.
  • FM: management theory and ethics are a large recurring base; finance requires both concepts/numericals and a separately maintained rules/current-changes notebook. The finance-current share varies sharply across years; do not extrapolate 2024 alone.
  • Suggested time split: ESI: 25% economics foundations, 15% social foundations, 45% policy/reports/schemes including environment, 15% integrated writing/recall. FM: 35% management/ethics, 30% finance foundations/numericals, 20% rules/current changes, 15% writing/recall. English: 40% essay, 30% precis, 30% comprehension. These are starting allocations, not PYQ mark estimates; adjust using timed-test errors.

Source limits and reproducibility

Memory-based questions are treated as the user's study evidence, but missing weights are not recovered by assumption. 2021 ESI prints Q11 where the first objective Q1 belongs. 2025 ESI Q11-14 have a mismatched recreated agriculture passage before MSME questions; classification follows the actual subquestions. 2025 FM Q28-30 explicitly warn that the reconstructed question/theme may not be exact. 2021 English has mismatched passage/subquestions and is unsuitable for detailed RC-content inference. No answer keys are used for this audit.
