    Adding next month's PDFs

    1. Put your PDFs anywhere inside this workspace. Keep previous editions.
    2. In Copilot, use /update-study-library (or ask "Update my study library from these PDFs") and provide their paths and edition month.
    3. Copilot first runs the full four-folder page scan, checks new/changed and uncited content, then produces short, paraphrased maps, tables, cards and dated updates. Low-text pages require OCR or visual review; extraction alone is not understanding.
    4. The local tool validates citations, appends the curated bundle and rebuilds this page. Refresh this page afterwards.

    No API key or hosting required. This HTML works offline, including search and maps. It does not upload PDFs, run AI by itself, or watch folders. Updates happen through Copilot in this workspace; the browser does not write back to the database.

    Source of truth: study_library\library.json. Previous database versions are saved in study_library\history on changed imports. Reimporting an identical bundle adds nothing. Different source observations remain visible, dated and flagged; a newer publication is not automatically more correct.

    Study loop: understand → organise → rebuild closed-book → apply → correct. Hide comparison columns; answer cards before revealing; redraw the map without looking. Review next day, next week, two weeks and one month, adjusting to recall.

    Changing facts need edition/reference period, scope and effective date. Even a single observation may be stale or wrong. A review flag can indicate a genuine update, a different measurement period or a source error—not necessarily a contradiction. Keep alternatives and verify official sources before memorising.

    Coverage is explicit in the PDF catalogue: topic packets synthesise selected cited pages. Monthly updates are selective and remain unverified; no edition is automatically complete.

    Each merge/build rescans all four evidence folders and refreshes the GA table. A scan can also be requested with study_library.py scan. Extracted page text and fingerprints stay local.
